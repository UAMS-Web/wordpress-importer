## Why this is a standing order

Raised on [#710](https://github.com/UAMS-Web/wordpress-importer-archive/pull/710), whose body carried "**I** followed its intent rather than its letter" and "an ambiguity **my own** edits worsened" — the first of which is still in it, so the example is checkable rather than remembered. A sweep of this repo's validation comments then found **72 of 82** needing a rewrite; all were corrected on 2026-08-24. The final pass over those 72 flagged 7 residual hits, of which **5 were verbatim quotations** of the documents under review — which is why the second carve-out below is a carve-out and not an oversight.

It is not confined to that sweep. A comment on [#720](https://github.com/UAMS-Web/wordpress-importer-archive/pull/720#issuecomment-5401303075), posted 2026-08-24, reads "the review's; **my own** probe could not have distinguished it".
